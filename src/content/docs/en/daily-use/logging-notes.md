The most powerful thing in Corpus isn't a metric — it's the connection between what you *do* and how your body *responds*. The behaviour log is how you build that connection. It's also the input that feeds your **personalised insights**.

## Five ways to log

### 1. The morning check-in

Three taps each morning, covered on its own page. The fastest, most predictable input — and the backbone of the insight engine.

### 2. Quick-tap chips

On your dashboard, Corpus shows suggestion chips based on your recent logging. Late meal yesterday? Took magnesium? Tap the chip. One touch, logged.

### 3. Free-form notes

Type a quick note any time. *"Had a huge steak dinner at 9pm."* *"Felt anxious all day."* You don't need to use specific terms — Corpus reads your note and extracts behaviour tags automatically (food types, timing, stress, recovery activities, symptoms). Just write naturally.

Notes are also useful for context the engine can't extract — a one-line description of why a workout felt off, what changed in your routine, anything you'd want a coach to know.

### 4. Voice memos

Hit the mic and dictate. Corpus runs on-device speech-to-text, then handles the result exactly like a typed note. Useful in the kitchen, after a workout, or anywhere typing isn't fast.

### 5. Photo logging

Snap a photo of a meal, your supplement stack, a workout, or anything relevant. Corpus generates a description and extracts behaviour tags from the image. It's the fastest way to log food — no typing, no calorie counting, just a photo. The original image stays on your device; only the model's analysis is stored.

### Plus: automatic detection

Some things Corpus logs for you. Naps, meditation sessions, mobility/yoga workouts, and menstrual cycle data are all detected automatically from Apple Health when present.

## What you can track

- **Food & drink** — late meals, heavy meals, high-sugar, high-protein, hydration, fasting, home-cooked vs. fast food.
- **Substances** — alcohol, late caffeine, cannabis, nicotine, with intensity (a glass of wine vs. a heavy night).
- **Sleep habits** — late screens, sleep mask, bedroom temperature, naps.
- **Recovery** — meditation, breathwork, stretching, massage, cold exposure, sauna, foam rolling.
- **Supplements** — magnesium, creatine, melatonin, vitamin D, omega-3.
- **Stress** — work and life, on a low/moderate/high scale.
- **Symptoms** — stomach issues, headache, low energy, soreness, anxiety, brain fog, skin breakouts, joint pain. These work *both* as things to log *and* as outcomes Corpus can find triggers for.

## How insights emerge

After two to three weeks of consistent logging, Corpus starts comparing days when you did something against days when you didn't. The output looks like:

- *"Late caffeine is associated with 28% less deep sleep."*
- *"Cold exposure days show higher next-day recovery."*
- *"High work stress is associated with 1.8× higher anxiety the next day."*

These are evidence from *your* data. They're not generic health tips — they're patterns Corpus found in the way *your* body responded to *your* habits.

## Tips that actually matter

- **Consistency beats perfection.** A short check-in every morning plus one or two photos a day is enough.
- **Use photos for food.** It's faster than typing and captures more detail.
- **Don't overthink it.** "Felt stressed" is a useful note. The engine extracts what it needs.
- **Log symptoms.** Tracking headaches, low energy or stomach issues lets Corpus find their triggers — sometimes that's the most valuable thing it surfaces.
