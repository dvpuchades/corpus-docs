The dashboard is the screen you'll spend the most time on. It's deliberately quiet: a comment, six tiles, a row of notes. Everything below it is detail you can drill into when you want.

## At the top: the daily coaching comment

Two to four sentences interpreting your overnight data, recent context, and what to focus on today. It's specific — it cites streaks ("third sub-target sleep night"), training-load ratios, recent insights — and it's written by the same coach you can chat with. If you tap on it, you can jump straight into chat with the comment as context.

## The six metric tiles

Each tile shows a clear *level*, not a cryptic number, plus a tiny trend line for context.

- **Sleep** — Excellent · Good · Fair · Poor.
- **Readiness** — Optimal · Good · Moderate · Low · Very Low. The "should I push today" tile.
- **Strain** — Minimal · Light · Moderate · High · Maximal. Cumulative load over the day.
- **Stress** — Relaxed · Mild · Elevated · High. Physiological, derived from HRV and resting heart rate trends.
- **Recovery** — Fully Recovered · Recovering · Underrecovered · Depleted.
- **Steps** — count, plus a 7-day breakdown and 30-day average.

Tap any tile to open its detail view: charts, history, contributing signals, what to do about a low or a high reading.

## Body composition (if you have a smart scale)

If a scale that syncs with Apple Health has logged a reading in the last 14 days, body composition appears below the metrics: weight, body-fat %, lean mass, plus 90-day trend charts and derived metrics like estimated BMR and TDEE. Recomposition (fat % dropping while weight is stable) is highlighted automatically — it's the kind of progress a single number on a scale would miss.

## Today's notes

A row at the bottom for today's logged behaviours and quick notes. Anything you log via check-in, quick-tap chips, free-form notes, voice or photo shows up here. Tap an entry to edit or remove it. Suggestion chips on the right offer one-tap logging for things you've logged recently.

## What to do with each level

- **Optimal/Good readiness, low/recovering recovery:** stick to the plan, push the planned session.
- **Moderate readiness:** listen — the plan will likely propose a slightly lighter session.
- **Low/Very Low readiness or Depleted recovery:** prioritise recovery; the plan auto-adjusts, and the coach will say so.
- **High strain with low readiness:** signal to ease off. Don't pile another hard session on top.
- **Elevated/High stress:** doesn't always mean you *feel* stressed — it can be undertraining showing through HRV, illness brewing, or poor sleep. Check today's notes and recent insights for clues.

## Going deeper

Every tile has a detail screen with charts, raw values where helpful, contributing factors, and any active insights related to it. The chat coach can also explain anything: tap into chat from the dashboard and ask "why is my readiness low today?" — it has the same data you do, plus the longer history.
