The week view is where the plan lives. Past days show a brief recap of your training and recovery; upcoming days show proposed sessions, each anchored to a "day ring" that lays out your sleep, work, and the available windows where Corpus can park a workout.

## How sessions get placed

Corpus reads three things to decide what each day looks like:

1. **What your goal needs from this week.** A marathon-build week looks different from a deload, looks different from a recomposition phase.
2. **How recovered you are.** Readiness and recovery for the past few days nudge intensity up or down.
3. **What your day actually looks like.** Sleep blocks, work blocks, anything you've marked busy. Sessions are placed in the gaps.

The result is a week of sessions that fit, with intensity calibrated to where you are right now — not where the textbook says a week-15 marathon-block should be.

## Adjusting the plan

You have several ways to change it:

- **Accept** the proposed session. Done.
- **Swap** a session for an alternative — Corpus offers a few that match your goal and load budget.
- **Plan your own.** Pick a workout type, duration and intensity; Corpus fits it into the load picture.
- **Mark a day busy.** Block off available hours; the plan reshuffles around it.

## Ask the coach to change it

This is the part most users miss: **the chat coach can edit your plan directly.** Open chat and ask:

- *"Move tomorrow's intervals to Friday and give me an easy run instead."*
- *"I'm travelling Wednesday — replace the gym session with something I can do in a hotel room."*
- *"I'm feeling rough; can we drop intensity for the rest of the week?"*

The coach has read access to your plan and write access to modify it. It looks up the relevant days, makes the edits, and tells you what changed. Same with goals — ask it to update your goal and it does.

## What the day ring shows

Every upcoming day has a 24-hour ring. From outside in:

- **Sleep** — your typical sleep window for that day of the week.
- **Work** — pulled from your work schedule (which you can edit in profile).
- **Available** — the gaps where Corpus is allowed to put a session.
- **Busy blocks** — anything you've explicitly marked as off-limits.

You can drag a busy block onto any day to cut into available hours. Useful for travel days, family days, or days where you just don't want to train.

## Past days

Past days in the week view show what actually happened: workouts logged from your watch, a brief AI-generated recap, and how each session compared to the plan. Tap any past workout for the full detail view — heart-rate zones, splits, route, and the post-workout insight.

## Training load: acute vs. chronic

In the background, Corpus is tracking your **training load ratio** — your last 7 days versus your last 28. When the ratio climbs above 1.3, you're ramping fast and injury risk goes up; when it sits low, you're not pushing enough to make progress towards the goal. Both surface as nudges in the coaching comment, and both shape what next week looks like.
