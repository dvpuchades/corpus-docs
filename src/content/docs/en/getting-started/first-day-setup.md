Corpus needs three things to be useful: access to your Apple Health data, a goal, and a few mornings of consistency. The first two take five minutes. The third happens by itself.

## 1. Install and open

[Download Corpus from the App Store.](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6761331575) iOS 26 is required (the app uses recent HealthKit APIs that older versions don't expose). Apple Watch Series 4 or later gives you the full picture; an iPhone alone still works, but readiness and recovery will be coarser without HRV and continuous heart rate.

## 2. Grant Health permissions

When you first open Corpus, iOS asks which Apple Health categories you want to share. Corpus shows what each one is for in plain language — none of it is opaque. The categories Corpus reads:

- **Heart rate & HRV** — for readiness, stress, and recovery.
- **Sleep stages** — for sleep quality, debt, and nap detection.
- **Activity & workouts** — for strain and training load.
- **Respiratory rate, wrist temperature, blood oxygen** — used when available, for finer recovery signals.
- **Mobility** — walking speed and asymmetry, captured passively by your iPhone.
- **Body composition** — weight, body-fat %, lean mass — from any smart scale that syncs to Apple Health.
- **Mindfulness** — meditation and breathing sessions, used for automatic behaviour detection.
- **Menstrual cycle** — flow data, used to factor cycle phase into recovery and readiness.

You can deny anything you don't want shared and still use the app — the missing signals just won't feed into the metrics they would have improved. You can change permissions later in *Settings → Health → Apps*.

## 3. Set a first goal

Corpus is goal-driven — not "be vaguely fit", but a concrete thing it can plan towards. Examples:

- "Half marathon on 12 October."
- "Sleep more consistently."
- "Recomposition over the next 12 weeks."
- "Less work stress, better evenings."

Pick one. You can change it any time, and the plan rebuilds itself.

## 4. Wear your Apple Watch

The more data the watch collects, the sharper Corpus gets. If you sleep with the watch on, you get full sleep staging and overnight HRV; if you don't, Corpus falls back to iPhone-detected sleep windows.

## 5. Check in tomorrow morning

The morning check-in is three short questions about yesterday — caffeine, stress, how you slept, that kind of thing. It takes about ten seconds. Those answers are what turn raw biometrics into *your* personalised insights over the next few weeks.

## What to expect

- **Day one:** your first metrics and a coaching comment.
- **First week:** the weekly plan starts adapting to your real schedule and recovery.
- **Two to three weeks:** the first personalised insights appear — patterns Corpus has found between things you've logged and how your body responded.
- **A few months:** Corpus knows your body in ways a generic coach never could.
