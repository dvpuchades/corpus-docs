Your Apple Watch and iPhone quietly collect thousands of health signals every day — heart rate, sleep stages, activity, respiratory rate, wrist temperature, even mobility. The numbers are accurate, but on their own they don't tell you what to *do*: should you push hard today or rest? Is your sleep actually recovering you? Which habits are helping, and which are quietly costing you?

**Corpus answers those questions.** It reads your Apple Health data, distils it into six daily metrics with clear levels (not cryptic numbers), and writes a personalised weekly training plan that adapts to your schedule and your recovery. There's a chat coach that has the same context you do — and can update your plan when you ask.

## Where Corpus fits

- **Apple Fitness & Health** give you the data, but no plan.
- **Whoop, Oura, Garmin Coach** give you a plan, but want a strap or a ring on top of your watch — and around €30 a month.
- **Corpus** sits in the gap, at €3.99 a month — the price of a coffee in Ireland. Your Apple Watch is already enough. Your iPhone is already enough.

## What you actually get

- Six daily health metrics: **sleep, readiness, strain, stress, recovery, steps** — each with a clear level (e.g. "Recovery: Recovering") plus the trend.
- **Body composition** if you have a smart scale that syncs with Apple Health.
- A **weekly training plan** built around your goal — a marathon, a recomposition, better sleep — and reshuffled when life intervenes.
- A **chat coach** that knows your data, your plan and your history, and can change them when you ask.
- **Personalised insights** that find the patterns in your own logged habits — what actually moves your sleep, your recovery, your readiness.

## Who it's for

- People preparing for a race who want a structured weekly plan and proper recovery guidance.
- People who want to feel better day to day — sleep more, lift smarter, manage stress.
- Anyone who has looked at their health data and thought "this is a lot of numbers, but what does it *mean*?"
